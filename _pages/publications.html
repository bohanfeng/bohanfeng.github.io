这部分论文围绕一条共同主线展开：如何将复杂制造中的工艺质量要求，以具备原理性的方式前馈转化为机器人运动、姿态与交互策略，而不是把质量问题留到执行后再修正。总体上，现有工作可归入三条彼此支撑的研究主线：

  1. 工艺质量驱动的机器人行为生成
  2. 人机协同下的工艺认知与质量共调控
  3. 工艺物理嵌入的机器人加工与操作优化

The publications below revolve around a common theme: how process-quality requirements in complex manufacturing can be translated into robotic motion, posture, and interaction strategies in a principled way, rather than leaving quality as a downstream variable to be corrected after execution. Broadly, the work clusters into three interconnected lines:

  1. Process-Quality-Driven Robot Behavior Generation
  2. Human-in-the-Loop Process Cognition and Co-Regulation
  3. Process-Physics-Embedded Robotic Machining and Manipulation
