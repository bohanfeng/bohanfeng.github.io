这部分论文围绕一个统一主线展开：**如何将复杂制造中的工艺质量要求，前馈地转化为机器人运动、姿态与交互策略**，而不是把质量问题留到事后补偿。当前工作主要沿三条相互支撑的研究主线展开： 1. **工艺质量驱动的机器人行为生成** 2. **人机协同下的工艺认知与质量共调控** 3. **工艺物理嵌入的机器人加工与操作优化**
The publications below revolve around a common theme: **how process-quality requirements in complex manufacturing can be translated into robotic motion, posture, and interaction strategies in a principled way**, rather than leaving quality as a downstream variable to be corrected after execution. Broadly, the work clusters into three interconnected lines: 1. **Process-Quality-Driven Robot Behavior Generation** 2. **Human-in-the-Loop Process Cognition and Co-Regulation** 3. **Process-Physics-Embedded Robotic Machining and Manipulation**
