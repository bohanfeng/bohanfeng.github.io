这部分论文围绕一条共同主线展开：机器人制造中的质量不是下游误差项，而是耦合机器人—刀具—工件—工艺系统中的涌现结果。因此，核心任务不是事后修正质量，而是以前馈、原理性的方式把质量要求转化为机器人运动、姿态、交互与工艺决策。总体上，现有工作可归入三条彼此支撑的研究主线：

  1. 工艺质量驱动的机器人行为生成
  2. 人机协同下的工艺认知与质量共调控
  3. 工艺物理嵌入的机器人加工与操作优化

The publications below revolve around a common theme: quality in robotic manufacturing should be understood not as a downstream error term, but as an emergent property of coupled robot-tool-workpiece-process systems. From this perspective, the central task is not merely to correct quality after execution, but to translate process-quality requirements into robotic motion, posture, interaction, and process-aware decision making in a principled way. Broadly, the work clusters into three interconnected lines:

  1. Process-Quality-Driven Robot Behavior Generation
  2. Human-in-the-Loop Process Cognition and Co-Regulation
  3. Process-Physics-Embedded Robotic Machining and Manipulation
