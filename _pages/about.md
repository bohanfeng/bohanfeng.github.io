**讲师，机器人与智能制造研究者**

您好！我是上海交通大学溥渊未来技术学院（GIFT）讲师，研究方向为**机器人与智能制造中的复杂决策与机理建模**——致力于将规划、学习与优化方法融合，赋予工业机器人在动态、不确定环境中的自主决策能力。

<div class="lang-en" markdown="1">

# Bohan Feng (Dylan Bo) | 冯波瀚
**Lecturer, Robotics & Intelligent Manufacturing Researcher**

Hi there! I am a Lecturer at the Global Institute of Future Technology (GIFT), Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My research focuses on **complex decision-making and mechanistic modeling for robots in intelligent manufacturing** — bridging planning, learning, and optimization to enable autonomous robot behavior in dynamic, uncertain industrial environments.

## <span class="lang-zh">研究问题</span><span class="lang-en">Research Question</span>

<div class="lang-zh" markdown="1">
如何使工业机器人发展出一种植根于机器人本体、工具、工件与制造过程耦合动力学的**具身且可泛化的智能**，从而在复杂物理制造环境中实现安全、自适应且精确的行动？
</div>

<div class="lang-en" markdown="1">
How can industrial robots develop **embodied and generalizable intelligence** grounded in the coupled dynamics of robot bodies, tools, workpieces, and manufacturing processes, enabling safe, adaptive, and precise action in complex physical manufacturing environments?
</div>
